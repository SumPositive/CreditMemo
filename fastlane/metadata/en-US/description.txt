Check your later debits

Deferin records and manages payments across every method — credit cards, debit cards, e-money and more. Version 2 was rebuilt from the ground up with the latest iOS technology for smooth performance and easy operation.

[Easy-on-the-eyes design]
Choose from four text sizes: Auto, Standard, Large, and Extra Large. The setting applies across the whole app — entry screens, selection sheets, even the help pages — for a display that's kind to your eyes.

[Debit management]
See "before debit" and "already debited" on one screen. Totals are shown across three periods: current, next, and future. Switch payment status with a single button.

[Fast entry]
When you enter a new payment, "Quote from History" automatically suggests similar past records. Unfilled fields such as label, payment method, and tags are filled in together, cutting the number of entry steps dramatically.

[Recording payments]
Record amount, date used, payment method, label, tags, and notes all at once. For recurring payments, you can also use repeat settings.

[Organize with tags]
Set multiple tags freely to categorize. Combine them with filters by card, by date, or "has blanks."

[Data management]
Supports export and import in JSON format. Use it to migrate data when you change devices or to keep an external backup.

Great for people who:
- Juggle several cards and find managing debit dates a hassle
- Want simple, hands-on management without automatic linking
- Are looking for an app with large, easy-to-read text
