What's New in 2.9.1
- New Payment: If a payment with the same date and amount already exists, you're asked to confirm before saving
- Amount keypad: Added a confirm button at the bottom right. As before, simply closing the keypad also applies your input
- Pay Methods: A method that has payments is now kept as "Closed" instead of being deleted, so you can still see its history. Deleting a closed method also deletes all of its payments
- Debit Statement: Unpaid and paid items are now shown together. Marking an item as paid no longer removes it from the list
- Tags: You can now choose how to pick tags: "Select & Done" or "One Tap"
- Payment List: When a payment has no label, the first line of its notes is shown instead
- Debit Status: Fixed paid items sometimes appearing as unpaid
