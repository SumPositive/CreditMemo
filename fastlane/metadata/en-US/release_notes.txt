What's new in 2.6.0
- Added "Frequent Payments" as an input aid for a new payment.
　- Pick a frequently used label to fill in the payment method and tags at once.
　- If you've used the same amount three or more times for a label, the amount is filled in too.
　- You can switch to the classic "Copy from Payment List" or "None" in Settings.
- New Payment by Voice now fills in the method, tags, and (for amounts used three or more times) the amount from the label as well.
- When paid records older than three years pile up, we'll suggest tidying up your data.
　- It exports before deleting, so you can restore by importing.
- In Payment Status, unpaid items older than one year are flagged with a caution icon.
　- Tap the icon to show those older unpaid items.
